Direction A — Clean Minimal

Pure whites, warm off-whites, generous space, soft shadows. Approachable for non-technical users. GOV.UK / NHS digital feel. Recommended for building site tradespeople.

Direction B — Utility / Trade

Green or blue accents, dense functional layouts, large touch targets. Language of Jobber, Xero, ServiceTitan. Feels like it was built for the job.

Direction C — Dark / Contrast

Near-black backgrounds, white text, vivid accents. OLED-optimised. Best outdoor sunlight readability on modern devices. Premium, technical feel.

Dark / High-Contrast Utility

Pure black or deep navy backgrounds with white text and vivid accent colours. Popular with Starlink, Apple Fitness, Peloton. On OLED screens, this can outperform light UIs in direct sunlight on a building site. Communicates precision, technology, and seriousness.

☀️
Outdoor readability advantageOLED dark mode can be more legible in direct sunlight than white backgrounds. If most users are on modern iPhones or Samsung devices with OLED screens, this direction has a real practical advantage.

Step-by-Step Wizard Flows

Multi-step forms with clear progress indicators — pill chains, step counters (3 of 4), filled progress bars, or numbered section lists. Pick one progress indicator style for consistencyThe client should choose between step pill chains (Chime), filled progress bars (Dave/KOHO), "X of N" text counters (Turo), or numbered section lists (Hims). Mixing styles adds confusion.
